Add your AirKing Standing Fan to Homey and control it when you're away from home via Homey's built-in Infrared transmitter. You can also use your fan in Flows. For example: use a Flow to turn on your fan automatically when the outside temperature rises above 25°C, so your home is stays comfortable. This app also works on fans which aren't WiFi-enabled, because it simply replicates the remote control. For WiFi-enabled fans, using the Tuya app is recommended.
